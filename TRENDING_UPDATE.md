# Current US Trending Discovery Update

The pipeline explains current US/Hollywood movie and international anime topics.

1. Discovery uses the US YouTube mostPopular chart plus Film & Animation signals, with no fixed franchise keyword list.
2. Gemini selects one current candidate using trend score, recency, engagement, and explainability.
3. The selected candidate is the canonical source subject for research, script, visual queries, and metadata.
4. Only MOVIE and ANIME classifications pass; movies require clear US/Hollywood relevance.
5. Topic-lock validation protects the selected subject while allowing natural title/description rewrites.
